Sort a Telegram channel into Eagle and Karakeep

  1. In Telegram Desktop, open the channel → ⋮ → Export chat history.
  2. Tick Photos, Videos and Files; choose format JSON.
  3. Click Open export… and select the exported folder (the one with result.json).

You decide where every post goes. Nothing is sent until you press a button.

Settings

Saved on this computer only, in .eagle-telegram-sorter.json in your user folder.
