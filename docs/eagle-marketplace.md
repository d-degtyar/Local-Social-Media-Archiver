**Name:** Local Social Media Archiver (27 characters; limit is 30 characters / 6 words)

**Keywords (max 6):** social media, instagram, linkedin, reddit, reference, browser

**Short description:**
Save posts from Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, and Reddit into Eagle with one click, including the caption, author, and original link.

**Description:**
Local Social Media Archiver is a free, open-source Chrome extension that sends images, carousels, and videos from the post you are viewing straight into your Eagle library. Each item keeps the original post URL, the author as a tag, and the caption as its annotation.

This plugin is the setup companion: it checks that Eagle is ready to receive posts and walks you through installing the extension.

- Instagram posts, carousels (up to 20 items), and Reels
- LinkedIn images, carousels, and videos
- Threads and Reddit posts, including full Reddit galleries
- "I feel lucky" mode for other sites

Private by design: no account and no server. The extension talks to Eagle only through its local API on your computer.

Install the extension free from the Chrome Web Store: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/local-social-media-archiv/hbjcghkjdbmphmbgkjkhncmihcbhenjj
